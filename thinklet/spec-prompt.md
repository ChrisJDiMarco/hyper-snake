# Hyper Snake - original Thinklet spec

The prompt this panel was built from (Thinklet panel id `wrrl5roo`).

---

Hyper Snake: the best, most jaw-dropping game of Snake ever made, playable right in the panel. Make it look and feel like a premium arcade game, not a toy. LOOK: real 3D. Use WebGL (hand-written, no external libraries) for a tilted, perspective neon grid arena floating in a dark synthwave void, with a slowly drifting starfield and a subtle camera sway that follows the snake. The snake is a glossy, glowing 3D tube with a lit head, eyes that look where it is turning, and a body that ripples and pulses with the beat. Food is a spinning, glowing gem that bobs and casts colored light on the floor. JUICE: every bite sets off a particle burst, a quick screen shake, a chromatic flash, and a floating +score popup. The snake gets faster and the arena's color palette shifts every level. Combo multiplier for quick eats, shown with a big animated counter. Occasional power-ups: slow-mo (time bends, everything goes blue), ghost (pass through yourself), and magnet (pulls in gems). Death is dramatic: the snake shatters into glowing shards in slow motion, then a stylish game-over card with score, best score and a big Play Again button. SOUND: punchy synthesized sound effects and a driving synthwave beat made with the Web Audio API (no files), with a mute toggle that is remembered. CONTROLS: arrow keys and WASD, swipe on touch, space to pause. Buffer quick turns so tight moves feel fair, and never allow reversing into yourself. A 3-2-1 countdown at the start. Title screen with an animated logo and the best score. Save the high score and the top 5 runs with cabinet.save/cabinet.load so they persist. Respect cabinet.reducedMotion by toning down shake and flashes. Must run at a smooth 60fps and scale crisply to the panel size. Design it as a polished, interactive app panel (scene.interactive = true).
